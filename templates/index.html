Deep Component Reconstruction for Person Re-ID

🧩 DCR-ReID System Architecture & Workflow

Implementation of the research workflow presented in Slide 6 of 23CSE373 - Computer Vision and the base paper DCR-ReID: Deep Component Reconstruction for Cloth-Changing Person Re-Identification.

1
Input Images
Surveillance RGB images resized to standard 256×128 Re-ID aspect ratio.
→
2
Preprocessing
Random Crop, Horizontal Flip, Color Jitter, and Random Erasing occlusion simulation.
→
3
Lightweight Backbone
Residual CNN backbone preserving spatial resolution with stride-1 in stage 4.
→
4
CRD Module
Component Reconstruction Disentanglement: separates clothes-irrelevant and clothes-relevant features.
→
5
DAD Module
Deep Assembled Disentanglement: Dual channel and spatial attention suppressing clothing noise.
→
6
BNNeck & Retrieval
Global Pooling, 1D BatchNorm neck, L2 normalized 512-D cosine distance matching.

Clothes-Irrelevant Features (Preserved & Amplified)

  • Face & Head Structure
  • Body Shape & Proportions
  • Human Structural Contour
  • Height & Limb Aspect Ratios

Clothes-Relevant Features (Disentangled & Suppressed)

  • Shirt & T-Shirt Color
  • Pants & Shorts Hue/Pattern
  • Backpacks & Handbags
  • Transient Accessories & Occlusions
