🧩 DCR-ReID System Architecture & Mathematical Formulation

Full implementation of DCR-ReID: Deep Component Reconstruction for Cloth-Changing Person Re-Identification (IEEE TCSVT 2023) combined with the lightweight edge architecture presented in 23CSE373 - Computer Vision.

1
Lightweight Backbone
Residual ConvNet (3.2M params) extracting deep convolutional feature map $P_i$.
→
2
CRD Module (CR Branch)
Decomposes $P_i = P_i^- \oplus P_i^+ \oplus P_i^t$ and reconstructs visual component masks with 4-block decoders $\psi^v$.
→
3
DAD Module (CI Branch)
Assembles $G_i = F_i^+ \oplus F_{ai}^- \oplus F_i^t$ with shuffled clothes and applies dual attention recalibration.
→
4
Dual BNNeck & Loss
Identity Classifier $C_{ID}$, clothes classifier $C_P$, hard triplet ranking, and label-smoothed CE.
→
5
Invariant Inference
Directly removes clothes-relevant features $F_i^-$ to perform robust cloth-changing matching.

📐 Mathematical Formulation (DCR-ReID Paper Equations)

Channel Decomposition (Eq. 8):
$P_i = P_i^- \oplus P_i^+ \oplus P_i^t$
$P^-$: Clothes | $P^+$: Body/Face | $P^t$: Contour
Reconstruction Loss (Eq. 10):
$L_R = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{v \in \{-, +, t\}} |T_i^v - Y_i^v|_1$
Feature Assembly (Eq. 16):
$G_i = F_i^+ \oplus F_{ai}^- \oplus F_i^t$
Shuffles clothes $F_{ai}^-$ across batch instances
Unified Total Loss (Eq. 19-20):
$L = L_{ID} + L_{triplet} + L_C + L_R$
$L_C = L_c + L_{ca} + \alpha L_{ac} + \gamma L_{ID}'$

Clothes-Irrelevant Features (Preserved & Amplified)

  • Face & Head Structure ($Y^+$)
  • Body Shape & Proportions ($Y^+$)
  • Human Structural Silhouette Contour ($Y^t$)
  • Height & Limb Aspect Ratios

Clothes-Relevant Features (Disentangled & Suppressed)

  • Shirt & T-Shirt Color ($Y^-$)
  • Pants & Shorts Hue/Pattern ($Y^-$)
  • Backpacks & Handbags
  • Transient Accessories & Occlusions
